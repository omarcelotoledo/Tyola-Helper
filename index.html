Tyola+ (AI)

Tap the + on the top bar to turn Tyola+ on or off. When it's off, Tyola works like a smart speaker: ready-made answers, timer, time, dance, walk and PC commands.
Uses Claude from your own account. The first question asks you to allow it. This only exists inside the Claude app; on the website, switch to “API key”.
The key never goes into the code or GitHub. It stays only on this device.

Pictures

Say “draw a cat astronaut” or “make a picture of a pizza dragon”. AI pictures come from pollinations.ai, a free service: one picture every 15 seconds, and it can add a small logo. Pictures work even with Tyola+ off; with Tyola+ on she writes a better description and remembers them.

Walk (mBot)

She counts 3, 2, 1, “Go!”: start the mBot on “Go!”. Saying “let's go for a walk” works too. Tap the screen to stop.
With the phone on the mBot, she feels the ride: looks into the turns, gets excited when the car moves, jumps on bumps, and the road only rolls while the car is really moving. Shake the phone to make her dizzy! Works on the website only (phone or tablet).

How to talk to Tyola

Inside the Claude app, the iPad/iPhone doesn't let Tyola use the microphone directly. But you can talk to her with the keyboard microphone (dictation):
  1. 1Tap Tyola's screen. The text box opens and the keyboard shows up.
  2. 2Tap the 🎤 on the keyboard, next to the space bar.
  3. 3Talk normally. The words appear by themselves.
  4. 4Tap Send. Tyola answers out loud.

No 🎤 on the keyboard?

Go to Settings › General › Keyboard and turn on Enable Dictation. If the keyboard is floating or split, pinch it back to normal and the 🎤 shows up.

I want the direct microphone, no keyboard

That doesn't work inside the Claude app. Open Tyola's website in Safari instead. There, tapping the screen uses the microphone directly.

If Safari blocks it: tap aA next to the address › Website Settings › Microphone › Allow, and keep Siri turned on in Settings.
